
==============================================================================
  OPPORTUNISTIC ROUTING: PACKET-LEVEL SIMULATION OF 4 NETWORKS
==============================================================================
  Purpose
    Check by simulation that the theoretical ETX of each network equals the
    average number of transmissions needed to deliver one packet from the
    source A to all of its destinations.

  Method (the same for every network)
    1. A broadcast to k receivers has 2^k reception cases. Their probabilities
       come from the link probabilities and must sum to 1.
    2. The cases are laid end to end on the number line [0, 1); each case gets
       an interval as long as its probability.
    3. For every transmission a random number r is drawn with
       randomgeneration() (ran2, Numerical Recipes). The case whose interval
       contains r is what happens in that transmission.
    4. Every node that receives the packet keeps a copy. The next sender is
       taken from the forwarding table of the ETX analysis: the holder with
       the lowest remaining ETX. If nobody new receives, it sends again.
    5. This repeats until every destination has the packet. The number of
       transmissions is counted, averaged over all packets and compared
       with the theoretical ETX.

  Settings
    Packets per network : 10000
    Random seed         : 42  (draw k passes k x 42 to randomgeneration())
    Link probabilities  : examples/case1.txt ... case4.txt
    Step-by-step log    : on (STEP 2 of each network)
